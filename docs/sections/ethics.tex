\section{Ethics and Broader Impact}
\label{sec:ethics}

This section addresses the ethical considerations, dataset provenance, and algorithmic transparency measures associated with this research framework.

\subsection{Dataset Provenance and Content Integrity}
Our experimental evaluation relies entirely on an open-source, publicly accessible dataset compiled from scraped question titles on the Stack Overflow platform. 
Because the corpus is strictly restricted to high-level technical inquiries and programming titles rather than user account profiles, it contains no personally identifiable information. 
Consequently, no secondary data anonymization pipelines were required. 
Furthermore, a rigorous manual review identified no systemic demographic or sociocultural biases within this technical text domain that could skew or harm the validity of our downstream clustering evaluations.

\subsection{Declaration of Generative AI Assistance}
In accordance with contemporary academic integrity standards and institutional disclosure guidelines, we explicitly declare the strategic utilization of generative artificial intelligence (AI) tools throughout this project's lifecycle. 

Large Language Models (LLMs) were leveraged under strict human supervision across three distinct domains:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Conceptual Brainstorming and Troubleshooting:} 
    Generative tools were utilized as an analytical sounding board to brainstorm architectural alternatives and troubleshoot complex structural bottlenecks that emerged during the pipeline's development phases.
    \item \textbf{Software Development Support:} 
    Automated code-generation tools were utilized to accelerate script compilation, optimize syntax for our bootstrapping algorithms, and refine data-parsing pipelines. 
    \item \textbf{Manuscript Synthesis and Refinement:} 
    Generative text assistants were employed during the drafting phase to enhance grammatical precision, eliminate typographic errors, and assist in restructuring complex prose into a cohesive, formal academic tone.
\end{enumerate}
Crucially, all primary conceptual designs, final algorithmic architectures, simulation parameter boundaries, and empirical interpretations were conceived exclusively by the human authors. 
The generative models functioned purely as structural, technical, and developmental optimization assistants, and all final outputs were rigorously audited, verified, and edited for factual validity prior to inclusion.
